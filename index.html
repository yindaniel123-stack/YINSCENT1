    Our story

    Because living beautifully is also breathing beautifully.

    A home transforms when the air tells stories — with scented candles and tealights that fill every corner with warmth.

    YINSCENT began with a simple belief: scent is memory. A single flame can recall a summer orchard, a linen-fresh morning, or the calm of lavender at dusk.

    We craft premium wax candles and scented tealights in signature aromas — from crisp apple and romantic rose to fresh ocean breeze and soothing jasmine. Each piece is designed for a clean, long-lasting burn and an atmosphere that feels unmistakably like home.

    • ✦ Premium quality wax
    • ◷ Long-lasting burn
    • ❧ Signature aromas
    • ⌂ Perfect for any space

    Small daily rituals matter. Lighting a candle at the end of the day is our invitation to pause, unwind, and breathe beautifully.
